A Bayesian twin of the Kelmarsh wind farm

Six Senvion MM92 turbines, open 10-minute SCADA data 2019-2021, unmodified. For every turbine and day the twin estimates hidden health parameters (efficiency, anemometer error, static yaw offset, derating, thermal resistance of the gearbox and generator bearings) with credible intervals, the probability of each fault type, the energy each cause costs and which inspection is worth most. Faults are judged against the turbine's own healthy behaviour in 2018.

Fault probabilities

Posterior probability that a fault of each type is present and material (worth acting on). Dashed: alarm threshold 0.9. Vertical lines: related messages in the turbine's status log (hover for text).

Health parameters

Posterior mean and 90 % credible interval, relative to the turbine's healthy behaviour in 2018.

Energy by cause

Whole farm. Potential: the energy the turbines would have produced, healthy and available, from their neighbours' wind and the wake model. Blue steps: health causes estimated by the twin (posterior mean). Value at an assumed £50/MWh.

Inspection ranking

Expected avoided loss of inspecting now and fixing what is found, over a 180-day horizon (until the next scheduled service), net of inspection (£500) and repair costs; EVPI: the part of it that comes from resolving uncertainty. Energy at £50/MWh; bearing cooling faults priced by an illustrative failure risk. All costs are assumptions (README). Negative: monitoring is cheaper than sending a crew.
